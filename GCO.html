I've been using SafeSpace for a couple of weeks now, and it's a great app for students who need mental health support. The platform is very easy to navigate, and I love how you can easily find and contact Peer Facilitators. The anonymity feature is really reassuring, especially when you're opening up about sensitive topics.

I've used the chat feature a few times and felt completely supported every time.

The Peer Facilitators and counselors are available, but I've experienced some delays in getting responses during peak hours.
